## Verification log
Checked 2026-10-11 against primary sources only.

1. FAA PFC page: "PFCs are capped at $4.50 per flight segment with a maximum of two PFCs charged on a one-way trip or four PFCs on a round trip, for a maximum of $18 total." Projects: "enhance safety, security, or capacity; reduce noise; or increase air carrier competition." The page doesn't mention terminals, so that part is removed. "Per enplanement" changed to the FAA's "per flight segment".
2. John Wayne Airport Key Provisions: "Phase 2: 11.8 MAP through December 31, 2025 Phase 3: 12.2 MAP from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2030 IF 11.21 MAP is not served between January 1, 2021 and December 31, 2025 OR 12.5 MAP … IF 11.21 MAP is served … and certain mitigation measures are met." FAQs: amendments "extended the agreement through December 31, 2030"; the curfew obligation runs "through 2035". Corrected because 12.5M is conditional, not automatic.
3. John Wayne Airport Reports & Resources: "prohibits commercial departures between 22:00 and 07:00 (08:00 on Sundays) and commercial arrivals between 23:00 and 07:00 (08:00 on Sundays)." Corrected to the exact hours, with the Sunday rule and arrivals added.
4. LAWA 2025 Aviation Activity Analysis: about "73.71 million annual passengers" in 2025, "a 3.76% decrease" on 2024 (10-Year Summary: 73,709,594). LAWA's 2019 release: record "88.1 million" (88,068,013), beating the 2018 high of 87.5M. Caveat: lawa.org blocks automated downloads, so these figures were read from LAWA's own documents through a search index, not opened directly. Open the 10-Year Summary in a browser to confirm before the call.
5. SCAG 2024 technical report: "Despite an estimated 22 percent of the air passengers in the SCAG region coming from Orange County, SNA only served 11 percent of the air passengers in the region." No primary source gives a count of LAX passengers from Orange County, so the 9.9M figure is dropped. The 22% estimate is AECOM's (2015) trip-origin analysis.
6. FAA JO 7110.308B, Appendix A: "SFO 28L / 28R … 750". FAA SFO Capacity Profile (2019): visual side by side "104-107 operations per hour"; instrument "28/01 Intrail … 76-78" and "28RT Intrail … 71-74". "7110.308 allows for reduced separation between SFO's closely spaced parallel arrivals … only applied at SFO in Instrument conditions." Corrected: the drop is about a quarter to a third (104→78 is 25%; 107→71 is 34%), not half. These are total operations (arrivals and departures), not arrivals only. A 2001 San Francisco budget analyst report also says that in instrument conditions "the FAA requires airports with such narrow runways to operate with only one of their two arrival runways".
7. San Francisco Board of Supervisors report: BCDC "issues Bay fill permits under the McAteer-Petris Act"; Proposition D (6 Nov 2001) requires "voter approval for any City project involving more than 100 acres of fill in San Francisco Bay … all of the runway build alternatives under consideration require more than 100 acres of Bay fill." FAA 1999 notice: "placement of fill in the San Francisco Bay (up to approximately 1,500 acres)". The 15:1 wetlands swap appears only in a trade journal, so it is dropped.
8. ANC release, 17 Apr 2026: "ranked the #1 cargo airport in the United States and #3 in the world … according to the latest rankings from Airports Council International World". The Jan 2026 handbook says "#4 globally" and "700+ wide-body freighter operations per week". The sources disagree on #3 vs #4, so we use the newer release; both are top 5. The only share figure found was a 2019 ANC claim of 79% of Asia–North America freighters. That page is no longer online, so the 80% figure is dropped.
9. ANC release, 1 May 2025: "handling over 5.5 million passengers" (2024, ANC alone). Handbook (Jan 2026): "ANC: … handling over 5 million passengers annually." No primary source gave a domestic or intra-Alaska split.
10. BTS PREZIP listing: the latest file is `…_2026_7.zip`, dated 9/21/2026. Other dates: 2026_1 on 5/1/2026, 2026_6 on 8/12/2026. Corrected "about 2 months" to "2–3 months", because the gap between a month's end and its file varies from about 6 to 13 weeks.
